## Assignment rules

- Assign one accountable owner per task and at least one reviewer.
- Do not assign tasks marked `BLOCKED / INPUT NEEDED` until the decision is recorded.
- Avoid giving different developers simultaneous ownership of the same high-conflict file.
- Pair a domain/persistence owner with a QA owner for DATA, AUTH, and CORE mutation tasks.
- Record branch/PR links in `PM_DASHBOARD.md`; keep this table focused on ownership and dates.
